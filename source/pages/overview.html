The 30-second cycle

What the watchdog does, every half minute

  1. 1HeartbeatA dated line proves the service is alive.
  2. 2LicenceThe latest time seen is recorded, the licence checked.
  3. 3Compliance checkEvery applied control is read and compared.
  4. 4RestoreAnything that differs is put back, and logged.
  5. 5Network policyThe firewall is held to the policy; refusals recorded.
  6. 6BackupThe protected copy of the master is refreshed.
