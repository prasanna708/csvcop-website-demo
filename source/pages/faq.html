Product & deployment

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 7 SP1 through Windows 11, 32- and 64-bit, with .NET Framework 4.5 or later, which is built into Windows 8 and later.

Does it need a server, a domain or the internet?

No. CSVCOP runs entirely on the workstation: no server, no domain, no internet, not even to activate. It works alongside a domain if you have one.

Will it get in the way of our instrument software?

The Windows controls act on the shell, not your applications. The few that reach every program say so in their description before you switch them on.

Can an operator stop it?

No. The service runs as SYSTEM and Windows restarts it if stopped; a standard user cannot end the session agent. Any change is put back within 30 seconds and logged.

Micro-segmentation

What does it do?

It decides which programs on the PC may talk to which machines, and refuses the rest: isolate the PC from its network, limit the internet to named programs, or both. Every refusal is recorded.

How do we know which rules a PC needs?

Switch on learning mode for a few days. CSVCOP records every connection the PC makes, and one click turns any of them into a rule.

Could a wrong rule cut the PC off?

Not for long. Before a tightening change, CSVCOP lists what will stop working. Once applied, it undoes itself unless it is committed within five minutes.

Does it need changes to our switches or VLANs?

No. The policy lives in the firewall built into Windows on each PC, held there by the CSVCOP watchdog. No driver, no appliance.

Compliance

What evidence does CSVCOP produce?

Up to nine reports, each a click from PDF with your logo and the Equipment ID: activity, control changes with their reasons, USB, the full audit trail and summaries — plus two network reports with Micro-segmentation.

Does CSVCOP validate our system?

No tool can. CSVCOP controls the workstation and records what happens there, which makes that part of your compliance simple to prove.

Can you map our auditor’s checklist?

Yes. Send it to us and we return it with the control numbers filled in; they are the numbers shown in the application.

Licensing

How do we buy CSVCOP?

Through our authorised distributor, SEK RAW Business Services: quotations, purchase orders, invoicing and renewals. Details on the pricing page.

How does activation work without the internet?

The PC shows a machine key; the licensing portal returns a signed licence key for it, which you paste back in. Any device with a browser can reach the portal.

Which modules does a licence include?

Every licence includes 21 CFR Part 11 Controls. Micro-segmentation is added for every workstation or a set number; customers give more PCs the module from their portal, within their seats.

What happens when a licence expires?

Warnings start 30 days before. After the end date everything keeps working for 14 days; then the controls and the network policy switch off until a renewed key is pasted. Nothing configured is lost.
