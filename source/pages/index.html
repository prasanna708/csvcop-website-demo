02 · Configure Controls

97 controls, each in plain words

Switch on what your SOP asks for. Each control says what it does and when it takes effect. Save & Apply asks for the password again, and switching a control off asks for the reason.

  • Only the controls that apply to this Windows version count
  • Takes effect: at once, at the next sign-in, or when Explorer restarts
  • Held in place by the watchdog every 30 seconds

03 · USB Control

USB storage, by device

Block USB storage, then let in exactly the devices your instruments need — recognised by their hardware identity, all the time or on a schedule.

  • The device ID read from the device that is plugged in
  • A weekday window per device, if it needs one

04 · Observed traffic

See what the PC really talks to

Learning mode records every connection the PC makes: the program, the address, the port, the protocol, how often and when. One click turns a line into a rule.

  • Allow this, allow the program to the internet, or allow all to one host
  • Blocked only, allowed only or everything; today, 7 or 30 days
  • Rules made from evidence, not guesswork

05 · Rules

Rules in plain words

Allow or block by program or Windows service, address, port and protocol. Presets cover a file share, SQL Server, a printer and Remote Desktop; a schedule limits a rule to its window.

  • In force: Yes, Idle, or Not now outside its window
  • A block rule works on its own, even with no restriction on
  • Every rule added, changed or removed is logged

06 · Restrictions

Five minutes to commit

Isolate the PC from its network, restrict the internet to named programs, or both. A tightening change is in force at once, and undoes itself unless it is committed within five minutes.

  • Before applying: the connections of the last 7 days that will stop working
  • A warning when the session looks remote
  • DHCP, DNS, time and Windows Update stay open

How it works

From a fresh Windows PC to a locked, segmented, audited workstation

An application decides, a service enforces, an agent guards the session, and the firewall built into Windows carries the network policy.

01 · INSTALL

Install and activate

One MSI per workstation. A licence key bound to that PC activates it, with no internet.

02 · LOCK DOWN

Switch on the controls

Choose the controls your SOP asks for; each says what it does. Save & Apply asks for the password again.

03 · SEGMENT

Decide the connections

Learning mode shows what the PC talks to. Make the rules from what was seen, apply the restrictions, commit them.

04 · PROVE

Print the evidence

Reports to PDF: the controls as applied, every change and who made it, the network policy and every refused connection.

Compliance

Built around what 21 CFR Part 11 asks you to show

CSVCOP supplies the workstation-level controls and writes the records. Your validation and SOPs remain yours — and become far easier to evidence.

Limited access

Windows policy for the workstation and password-policy, rate-limited CSVCOP accounts. § 11.10 (d)

Audit trails

Time-stamped entries with the account, written independently of the operator: tamper events, blocked attempts, refused connections. § 11.10 (e)

Operational checks

Controls and firewall rules that put themselves back every 30 seconds cannot drift between audits. § 11.10 (f)

Device checks

Removable-storage control with a whitelist by hardware identity. § 11.10 (h)
